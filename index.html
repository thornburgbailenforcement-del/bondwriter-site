About the practice

Thornburg's Private Investigations, Bonds & Bail Enforcement is based in Norwalk, Iowa and operates with a simple philosophy: tell clients the truth, do the work you say you’ll do, and document everything.

The focus is on a limited number of active files at any given time so that each case receives direct attention and deliberate planning. That means clear communication about what can and cannot be done, realistic timelines, and no sugar‑coating of risk or limitations.

If your situation involves a bond, a failure to appear, a missing or evasive individual, or a sensitive domestic matter, you can expect a straight conversation about options, constraints, and next steps.
